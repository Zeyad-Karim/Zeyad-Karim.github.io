## whoami

Hey, I'm **z3y4d** (Zeyad) — a cybersecurity enthusiast, CTF player, and penetration tester. I spend my time breaking into vulnerable machines, solving security challenges, and documenting everything I learn along the way.

This blog is where I publish writeups of vulnerable machines, CTF challenges, and web security labs. The goal is simple: take notes, share knowledge, and help others learn from the same boxes.
